title: "Your dashboard & registration status"

Your dashboard is home base: it shows every event you're registered for, what state each registration is in, and the single next thing you need to do.

![The dashboard with an in-progress registration](/participants/images/dashboard-in-progress.png)

## The status labels

Each event registration has one status:

- **Awaiting Participant** — the ball is in your court: finish onboarding or sign your waiver. The dashboard will show a **Continue registration** button.
- **Awaiting Parent** — you're done for now; your parent or guardian still needs to sign their forms. See [Your parent or guardian's part](/participants/08-guardian-consent).
- **Complete** — everything is signed and submitted. You're coming! 🎉
- **Withdrawn** — you've been withdrawn from the event. If you think this is a mistake, contact the organizers.

## Your event page

Click into any event for the full picture:

![The event page for a completed registration](/participants/images/event-hub-complete.png)

From here you can:

- see your **ticket and QR code** (and add it to your phone's wallet) — see [Your ticket & wallet passes](/participants/10-tickets-and-wallet-passes),
- check whether your **parent/guardian** has finished their part,
- view and **edit your travel details**,
- review all the **information you submitted**,
- sign any **new documents** the organizers add,
- read recent **messages** from the organizers.

<Note>
  **Something on your event page needs fixing but it's locked?** After submission most fields are read-only (travel is the exception). Email [attend@hackclub.com](mailto:attend@hackclub.com) and we'll update it for you.
</Note>
