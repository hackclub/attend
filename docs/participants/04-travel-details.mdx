---
title: "Adding your travel details"
---

The Travel step tells organizers when you arrive and when you leave, so they can plan pickups and make sure nobody is stranded. You fill it in during onboarding, and — unlike everything else — you can **keep editing it after you submit**, right up to the event.

![The Travel step of the onboarding wizard](/participants/images/onboarding-travel.png)

## What we ask for

- **Visa status** — whether you need a visa to attend, if your event asks.
- **Getting there (arriving)** — how you'll arrive in the event city.
- **Getting home (departing)** — how you'll leave.

For each direction, pick your mode of travel: **plane, train, bus, car, or other**.

## Flying? A few extra fields

If you pick **plane**, each flight needs:

- the **flight code** (like UA1234 — it's on your booking confirmation),
- the **departure and arrival airports** (start typing and pick from the suggestions),
- the **departure and arrival times**.

Attend checks the flight code against real flight data, so double-check it matches your booking exactly.

<Note>
  **Booked but don't have flights yet?** That's fine — you can come back. Enter what you know now and update it later from your event page.
</Note>

## Updating travel after you've submitted

1. Go to your **Dashboard** and open your event.
2. Find the **Travel** section and click **Edit**.
3. Make your changes and save.

![Editing travel details from your event page](/participants/images/travel-edit.png)

If organizers are missing your travel info, you might get a friendly reminder email — the link in it takes you straight to this page.

## Flying alone as an unaccompanied minor?

If your airline registers you as an unaccompanied minor (UM), there's a specific flow for that — see [Flying as an unaccompanied minor](/participants/05-unaccompanied-minors).
